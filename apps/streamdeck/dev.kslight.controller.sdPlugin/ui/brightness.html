Choose brightness

Turn to choose 1–100%; press to apply your configured color or native effect at that level. Preview starts at 50%, not the current lamp brightness.

Optional: combines rapid turns into the latest level, with one command at a time. Failure pauses automatic sending; check the hub, then press to deliberately send again. Dimming uses the configured action color/effect.

Credentials stay in your token file. Sent means the hub completed delivery; it does not confirm the lamp's physical state. Simulated means no lamp was changed.
